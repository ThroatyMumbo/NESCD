`cdi.pretty/Pimoroni_PGA2350.kicad_mod` and the `Pimoroni_PGA2350` symbol in
`cdi.kicad_sym` come from https://github.com/vurdalakov/kicad8-symbols-and-footprints
and are used here with modifications: four pins retyped to `power_in`, the
footprint property renamed to the local library, and a 3D model reference added.
